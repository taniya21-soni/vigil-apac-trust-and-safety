# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Synthetic & Manipulated Media in Civic Processes

**Policy Code:** SOP-TNS-CIVIC-001  
**Region Focus:** APAC / India  
**Enforcement Level:** Content Specialist Tier-2 / Tier-3 Escalations  

---

## 1. Purpose & Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the criteria for identifying, triaging, and adjudicating content that leverages synthetic, altered, or miscontextualized multimodal media (audio, video, imagery) to manipulate or disrupt democratic civic events, elections, and public order.

---

## 2. Policy Violations & Harm Criteria

Content violates the platform's Civic Integrity Guidelines if it contains manipulated or synthetic media creating an immediate, plausible risk of:

1. **Voter Suppression & Administrative Deception:**
   - Fabricating voting hours, polling station closures, ballot rules, or eligibility requirements.
   - Falsely claiming election cancellation or postponement.

2. **Deceptive Endorsements & Fabricated Statements (Voice Clones / Deepfakes):**
   - Synthesizing voices or video of candidates, electoral commission officials, or key figures stating they have dropped out, conceded, or changed allegiances.
   - Creating realistic synthetic audio of candidates admitting to criminal activity or electoral bribery during active campaigning or silence periods.

3. **Manufactured Evidence of Electoral Tampering:**
   - Presenting doctored footage as authentic proof of electronic voting machine (EVM) manipulation, ballot stuffing, or booth capturing.

---

## 3. The EDSA Matrix (Educational, Documentary, Scientific, Artistic)

An apparent violation may remain on the platform under an **EDSA Exemption** if it satisfies all three prongs below:

* **Prong A — Prominence of Disclosure:**
  The artificial or altered nature of the media is explicitly stated in the title, opening audio track, or a clear persistent visual overlay (not relegated solely to collapsed description fields).

* **Prong B — Valid Expressive Intent:**
  The media clearly functions as:
  - Satire or parody where an average viewer would not reasonably assume reality.
  - Investigative journalism reporting on or exposing the existence of a disinformation campaign.
  - Scientific analysis explaining synthetic voice/video technology.

* **Prong C — Public Safety Threshold:**
  Even with disclosure, content that directly incites imminent physical clashes, riots at polling locations, or physical obstruction of voters is **never** exempt under EDSA.

---

## 4. Operational Enforcement Tiers

| Tier | Enforcement Action | Criteria / Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 0** | **Approve (Safe)** | Genuine political speech, normal campaign debate, or full compliance with all three EDSA prongs. |
| **Tier 1** | **Demote + Fact-Check Context Card** | Authentic media presented with sensationalized, misleading context, but with low probability of direct real-world harm. |
| **Tier 2** | **Age-Gate + Quarantine Recommendations** | Graphic civic unrest or protest clash footage that has documentary news value but is unsuitable for minors. |
| **Tier 3** | **Immediate Platform Removal + Strike 1** | Deceptive AI voice clones or deepfakes alleging false election procedures, ballot tampering, or fabricated candidate concessions. |

---

## 5. Escalation Routing Protocols
* **P0 Escalation (SLA: < 15 minutes):** Viral content (>10,000 views/hr) or content uploaded within 48 hours of polling containing synthetic audio/video of public officials. Immediate notification to APAC Escalations Lead & Legal.
* **P1 Escalation (SLA: < 60 minutes):** Content targeting regional or municipal elections with moderate velocity.
* **P2 Routine Review (SLA: < 120 minutes):** Static miscaptioned archival footage or low-reach metadata disputes.
