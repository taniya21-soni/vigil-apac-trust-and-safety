### 1. Executive Summary
At 13:45 IST, an unverified account uploaded a 42-second clip purportedly showing a regional political candidate stating that voting procedures for tomorrow's polling phase had been suspended across 14 constituencies due to electronic voting machine (EVM) irregularities. Within 25 minutes, the video reached 140,000 views and 18,000 shares across external messaging apps.

---

- **Content ID:** `yt-vid-90283104-IN`
- **Technical Analysis:** Audio exhibits spectral anomalies consistent with an RVC (Retrieval-based Voice Conversion) synthetic voice model trained on public campaign speeches. Lip-sync discrepancies confirm AI alteration of archival 2022 press footage.
- **Triggered Policy Clause:** *YouTube Community Guidelines - Civic Integrity & Manipulated Media Section 4.2: Deceptive Practices related to Voting Processes.*
- **Evaluation of EDSA Defense:**
  - *Title:* "Breaking News: Voting Canceled!" (Deceptive framing)
  - *Description:* Contains no disclosure of satire, parody, or educational breakdown.
  - *Determination:* **EDSA exception fails on all three criteria.** High probability of direct voter disenfranchisement.

---

### 3. Risk-Impact Analysis
- **Option A: Full Video Removal & Strike 1 on Channel (Recommended)**
  - *Pros:* Stops viral spread immediately; eliminates offline voter suppression risk.
  - *Cons:* Potential political bias allegations from the channel's followers.
- **Option B: Restrict Recommendations & Add Informative Panel**
  - *Pros:* Maximizes platform neutrality optics.
  - *Cons:* Unacceptable real-world harm; 12 hours before poll opening, speed of distribution outpaces fact-check labels.
